# Pilot Production Budget — Markdown model
**AI Video Monetization Plan 2027 | Planning snapshot: 16 Nov 2026**

All euros are estimates, not quotes; exclude recoverable VAT, tax and Attila compensation. Assumes founders own capable computers and produce one 10-minute, 8–12 minute-target episode. Startup totals exclude professional advice, shown separately. Monthly bookkeeping/admin €100 is an unverified placeholder pending accountant quote. Vendor pricing and FX can change; verify before commitment. There is no dependable revenue before full YPP approval.

## B. One-time professional and contingency reserve
Set aside €500–€1,000 for HU/NL accountant/legal advice and written collaboration/IP/tax review; no quote was obtained and actual fees may exceed this. Balanced initial funding: pilot cash €1,660 + 15% contingency €249 + advisers €500–€1,000 = **€2,409–€2,909**. Recommended hard cap €3,000 before publishing; pause if a quote or required compliance work pushes it higher. No new-company formation cost is included. Taxes and FX are excluded; HUF equivalent = EUR amount × current documented exchange rate on transaction/reporting date, not a fixed forecast.

## C. Cash runway (zero-revenue stress case)
One video/month. Month 1 pilot cash €1,660; each of months 2–6 costs €610. Six-video cohort cash = €1,660 + 5×€610 + €500–€1,000 advice + €249 contingency = **€5,459–€5,959** before revenue/tax. Add 6×100h founder labor × €25 = €15,000; six-episode fully loaded cost ≈ €20,459–€20,959.

Twelve months/12 episodes: €1,660 + 11×€610 + €500–€1,000 advice + €249 contingency = **€9,119–€9,619 cash** before revenue/tax; add €30,000 imputed labor for 1,200h. This is a no-revenue runway stress test, not a spending recommendation. The stop/go gate is six videos/12 months, not a commitment to fund all twelve.

## D. Revenue and break-even sensitivities
Model formulas: ads = monetized monthly views × RPM / 1,000; total revenue = ads + other revenue; cash net = total revenue − monthly cash cost; labor-adjusted net = cash net − founder labor. RPM here is an assumption, not CPM and not a sourced benchmark. One video/month, balanced costs €610 cash and €2,500 labor (€3,110 loaded). Taxes, VAT, repayment/distribution to founders and platform eligibility are excluded.

These are break-even sensitivities, not campaign forecasts; paid-campaign views do not prove organic growth or qualify toward the watch-hour threshold.
