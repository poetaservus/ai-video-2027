# Decision & monetization correction
**Interim verdict: CONDITIONAL GO for a measured pilot only; NO-GO for a profitability claim or scale-up.** Existing €5 RPM sensitivity is not verified market data. At that assumption, legacy cost floors require 122,000 views/month for €610 cash or 622,000 for €3,110 including 100h×€25. These exclude AI generation cost and therefore understate required views. Actual threshold V = max(0, monthly fixed + episodes×AI-first episode cash + labor − other revenue) × 1,000 ÷ realized RPM. Before full YPP ad approval, use zero ad revenue.

Runway API output-only costs at assumed 40% usable yield are about $211 fully generative, $43.50 hybrid or $11.50 budget per 10-min episode (10); these are not complete budgets. At 100k views and assumed €5 RPM, gross creator revenue is €500 only after YPP acceptance. Do not compare net profitability until FX, subscriptions, rights, audio/editing, taxes and labor are priced. Therefore two founders' profit remains unproven, not disproven; the immediate test is a quality-checked hybrid pilot, followed by repeated audience evidence.

Existing plan’s 16 Nov 2026 YPP source review says originality/authenticity, channel-wide review and reused-content rules remain distinct from copyright permission. Synthetic/AI labels are not a blanket YPP ban; disclose realistic altered/synthetic depictions as applicable, following the source-reviewed YouTube Help page. Human reporting, script, fact-checking, visual direction, editing and QA should make episode-specific original contribution evident. A license does not guarantee monetization or resolve copyright ownership. Preserve prompts, source/claim records, asset/voice licenses and edit versions; check each tool's terms and current Studio rules before upload.
