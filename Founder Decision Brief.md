Authorize one tightly capped, quality-first pilot only after legal/accounting review and a written Attila–Roland agreement. Do not treat the venture as a proven income source or plan around ad revenue before YPP approval.

**Niche:** English-language animated business/technology decision case studies. Best qualitative score among eight screened niches; differentiation via sourced timelines, custom charts, and EU context. Demand, competitor views and RPM still need live validation; this is a reasoned hypothesis, not proof.

**Pilot:** “How BlackBerry Lost the Smartphone Race—And What It Misread.” Ten minutes; ~1,300 words; original diagrams/timeline, licensed AI narration, restrained stock/music. 8–12 minute adult/general audience. Tell a nuanced sourced story, not a one-cause myth.

**Tools:** Free DaVinci Resolve + Blender + Inkscape/Krita + Audacity; test ElevenLabs against local/free TTS before any paid plan. A 16 Nov 2026 page lists ElevenLabs Starter at US$6/month with commercial licensing; confirm current tier and terms before use.

## Capital and unit economics (assumptions)
Balanced: setup €1,050; monthly subscriptions €190; provisional bookkeeping/admin €100/month; variable €320/video. First pilot cash €1,660. Add 15% contingency (€249) and €500–€1,000 professional-review reserve: initial cash need ~€2,409–€2,909; hard cap €3,000. Advisor allowance is unquoted; pause if fees exceed it. Founder time: ~100h/pilot, imputed at €25/h = €2,500; loaded pilot ~€4,160, or €416/finished minute at 10 minutes. Monthly cost with one video = €610 cash plus €2,500 labor.

**View thresholds, not forecasts:** assumed €5 RPM means ~122,000 monetized monthly views to cover €610 cash and ~622,000 to cover cash plus labor. Before full YPP acceptance, ad income is €0. RPM estimate is a sensitivity input; actual results depend on audience, country, season, retention and ad suitability. Paid channel promotion is excluded from the €610 baseline (€0 default). A separately approved €150 test would raise that month to €760; at assumed €5 RPM, post-YPP cash break-even arithmetic would be 152,000 monetized views, not a forecast or justification to buy traffic. Google Ads prices are auction/account-specific; public rates are not assumed. Official YouTube terms state 55% of net Watch Page ad revenue under the module and 45% of each creator’s Shorts Creator Pool allocation, with no guarantee of earnings. See YouTube Advertising & Operating Costs.md for sourced mechanics and guardrails. No gross receipts equal founder take-home; calculate tax and Attila compensation only after advisor review of Roland’s exact tax/VAT position and Attila’s Dutch status.
